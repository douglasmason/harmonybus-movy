# MIDI Event Heads — Record X/Y, replay, and exact captured-loop Bake

This increment builds on the live sparse-safe MIDI heads, Field/Move/Launchpad
X/Y inputs and the shared remote touch surface. There is **no second scheduler,
synth, transport, automation file or grain-recipe store**.

## Record the motion you actually hear

1. In **Joint Play → Sources**, choose a separate, playing **Pitch Source**
   clip and **Rhythm Source** clip for a Bass, Comp, or Melody actor.
   Neither donor may be on the actor's own track. Set Alignment to
   **Event Heads**, Source Weaving On, and Follow Amount to 100%.
2. In **Joint Play → MIDI Event Heads**, choose live **Head 1 Position**
   and **Head 2 Position** sources (usually Field 1 and Field 2).
   Set **Record Head XY** to On. This explicit opt-in is necessary:
   ordinary Record with it Off still records the actor's Own material.
3. Arm **Record** on that actor, and play the performance with the native
   8×4/8×8 Field XY pad layout, Remote UI, or existing Field strips.
   The *existing* source reader continues sounding through the native
   MIDI/gate owner instead of switching back to the actor's Own notes.
4. At each **accepted donor rhythm attack**, the sequencer captures the
   exact normalized 14-bit X/Y head positions it used for that attack.
   Finger movement, stationary hold refresh, display repaint, empty donor
   steps and filtered rhythm notes do not create position keyframes.
5. Finish Record. The keyframes belong to the actor's **ordinary clip**,
   using the existing saved and Undoable `shared_events` timeline
   (kind 3). This is stepped musical-event automation, **not** audio-rate
   interpolation; it preserves higher than 7-bit coordinate precision.

Choose **Recorded X** and **Recorded Y** in the same two Head Position
selectors to replay those recorded coordinates. Other live Field inputs
may be disconnected or moving: playback uses only the saved clip events.
The original rhythm donor still owns attacks and note-off duration.
Source notes, chords, operation metadata and existing MIDI instruments
remain owned by their original paths. You can select Recorded X on
either head or combine one recorded axis with live Field, LFO or Clock.

The clip-owned event lane has a total capacity of 512 shared events
(including other shared context events). For exact Bake, record a complete
loop and keep its original timing and source route unchanged. If some
accepted attacks were not recorded due to capacity, clip relaunch or
unavailable donors, Bake refuses rather than interpolating fabricated notes.
Motion capture itself is non-destructive and a later pass can overdub
keyframes at the same ticks. Capture is only active when the selected
actor's playing and active clips match.

## Bake to a normal, editable MIDI clip

1. Finish Record and open **Clips → Fill** (the existing Clip Fill editor).
2. Select alignment **Recorded XY** on knob 6. Knobs 1 and 2 choose the
   actor and its *captured motion clip*. Knobs 3/4 are derived from the
   actor's saved Source Weaving route rather than independently editable.
   Knob 5 chooses the length; it must equal the recorded actor and rhythm
   donor loops.
3. Preview the source. It reads the saved X/Y events, the donor's note/chord
   onset groups, the plain rhythm donor's attacks and the actor's original
   head pattern, offset and source identity. No notes are regenerated by
   viewing the preview.
4. Tap an **empty or replaceable different clip slot on the same actor**.
   Leave the usual **Own content** choice selected, not Keep weaving.
   This uses precisely the existing Clip Fill SHA-checked source preview,
   destination fingerprint, atomic clip write and Undo transaction.
5. Launch the baked clip normally. It contains ordinary, editable note
   events and their native rhythm gates. The source route is switched to
   Own to prevent a second live reader from doubling attacks. Undo
   restores both the destination clip and the original source routing.

### Exact parity contract (intentionally restrictive)

Bake currently requires an entire matching **one-to-one, original-rate**
actor/rhythm loop, a plain note rhythm clip without time-variant triggers,
operations, automation, generators or render modifications, 100% follow
amount, and recorded coordinates for *every* rhythm attack. It rejects
different lengths, missing attack samples, routed expression or additional
operations/gestures/HB render automation and overflowing note capacity.
A melodic pitch source must have saved input-key intent. This makes the
contract auditable and avoids false claims that a complex live Field
performance was baked when some hidden, stochastic state was omitted.

The result is a reconstruction of the raw MIDI note-on/pitch/gate material
supplied to the normal Move/HarmonyBus output path. Harmonic rendering
will also depend on the shared key, tempo and instrument settings at
playback. Transient 14-bit Field motion is no longer required once the
baked clip is saved; the original captured motion remains independently
editable as recorded source events in its actor clip.

## Safety and verification

Ordinary Record with Head Motion off retains its old Own playback path.
Opted-in capture requires explicit independent pitch/rhythm donors and
cannot write back into its own source, or turn a previously captured
recorded position into a feedback recorder. A missing input falls back
to the accepted event cursor; invalid numeric samples are rejected.
The UI route settings retain their historical 7–32-field serialization;
only enabling motion capture adds the optional final field. All existing
per-note gate/stop/mute/retrigger rules apply.

Native regression coverage checks note/chord/gate parity between live and
recorded-position playback, same-duration baked clip playback, one-bar
event bounds, missing/muted donor samples, persistence, replay after
disconnect, stale source and destination signatures, rejected unsupported
source operations, invalid routes, and Clip Fill Undo. Compiled Move/
Launchpad controller and browser/ARM suites check that the new controls
do not break the shared UI and stock Move output chain.

**Still outstanding:** interpolation of sparse head curves between attacks,
non-plain Euclidean/generative donor Bake, full live-expression automation
and source operation replay, and physical Move/Launchpad timing, crackle
and pressure acceptance. Those remain separate #287 milestones, not
additional hidden schedulers.
