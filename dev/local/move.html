NuSaw audio · no Move device needed

Drag knobs vertically, or select one and use − / +.

Play: tap pads. Edit a clip: tap a step to add or clear a note; hold a step and play pads to change its pitches. Hold a step and turn Volume to change velocity. Undo: the curved arrow; Shift + Undo redoes. Note / Session switches views. Touch controls support simultaneous holds.

Session files

Stop before saving. Clips, Scenes/Song, HarmonyBus, NuSaw, LFOs, mix, Movy layout and preferences are included. This surface and Joint Play keep separate browser autosaves; export/import transfers engine sessions between them.

Audio & build

The real Movy UI, sequencer, HarmonyBus and Schwung chain host run in your browser. The device drawing comes from Schwung’s Move Mirror. NuSaw supplies audio. Move OS, stock instruments, device master volume, master FX, external MIDI and Move Set files are not emulated. Hold a track and turn Volume to edit that track’s mix. Use Monitor level for browser output. The page currently needs a network connection to reload.
