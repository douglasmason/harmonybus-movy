Play: tap pads. Edit a clip: tap a step to add or clear a note; hold a step and play pads to change its pitches. Hold a step and turn Volume to change velocity. Undo: Undo; Shift + Undo redoes. Note / Session switches views. Touch controls support simultaneous holds.

Audio & build

The real Movy UI, sequencer, HarmonyBus and Schwung chain host run in your browser. The enlarged display and control grid extend our HB/Movy browser interface. NuSaw supplies audio. Move OS, stock instruments, device master volume, master FX, external MIDI and Move Set files are not emulated. Hold a track and turn Volume to edit that track’s mix.
